I am a **Researcher at the University of Tsukuba, Japan**, working in computational imaging, inverse problems, and artificial intelligence for medical image reconstruction. I received my Ph.D. in Engineering from the University of Tsukuba in September 2023 under the supervision of Prof. [Hiroyuki Kudo](https://trios.tsukuba.ac.jp/researcher/0000000957). Before joining Tsukuba, I completed my M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees at [Sohag University](https://www.sohag-univ.edu.eg/ar/home-2/), Egypt.

My research focuses on reconstructing high-quality images from incomplete or limited measurement data, particularly in **sparse-view and limited-angle computed tomography (CT)**. I develop methods that combine imaging physics, numerical optimization, compressed sensing, and modern deep learning, with particular interest in **physics-guided learning, generative models, attention mechanisms, and data-consistency strategies**. I also work on electron tomography and remain interested in multimodal learning for medical imaging.

**Research interests:** CT Reconstruction · Sparse-View CT · Limited-Angle CT · Inverse Problems · Physics-Guided Deep Learning · Generative Models · Electron Tomography

## Research Direction

My work aims to make image reconstruction more accurate and reliable when measurements are sparse, incomplete, noisy, or otherwise constrained. A central theme is to integrate physical knowledge directly into learning-based reconstruction so that the resulting models remain faithful to the acquisition process while preserving fine image structure.

More broadly, I am interested in computational methods that connect **mathematical modeling, imaging physics, and artificial intelligence** for challenging inverse problems in biomedical and scientific imaging.

## Profile

I am a **Researcher at the University of Tsukuba, Japan**, working in computational imaging, inverse problems, and artificial intelligence for medical image reconstruction. My research focuses particularly on **sparse-view and limited-angle CT**, physics-guided deep learning, generative reconstruction, and model-based methods for recovering images from incomplete measurement data.

I received my Ph.D. in Engineering from the University of Tsukuba in 2023. My research has progressed from mathematical and optimization-based reconstruction methods, including level-set and compressed-sensing approaches, toward hybrid frameworks that combine imaging physics with modern deep learning.

My work has been supported by competitive research funding and fellowships, including the **JSPS Postdoctoral Research Fellowship** and **KAKENHI**, and has resulted in peer-reviewed journal and conference publications in medical imaging, inverse problems, and computational imaging.

## Selected Highlights

- Researcher at the **University of Tsukuba** since August 2026.

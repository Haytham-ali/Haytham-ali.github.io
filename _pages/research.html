--- layout: archive title: "Research" permalink: /research/ author_profile: true --- My research focuses on **computational imaging and inverse problems**, with particular emphasis on reconstructing high-quality CT images from incomplete projection data. I combine mathematical modeling, image-reconstruction physics, numerical optimization, and deep learning to develop methods for challenging acquisition settings such as **sparse-view** and **limited-angle CT**. A central theme of my work is to integrate learned image priors with explicit knowledge of the measurement process. Rather than treating reconstruction purely as an image-to-image mapping problem, I am interested in methods that incorporate projection-domain information, data-consistency mechanisms, attention, and frequency-aware representations. My broader goal is to develop reconstruction methods that are accurate, physically meaningful, and robust under practically relevant data limitations. [View Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}){: .btn .btn--primary } [View CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}){: .btn .btn--inverse } --- ## Research Directions ### Sparse-View CT Reconstruction Sparse-view CT reduces the number of acquired projections, which can lower acquisition burden but makes the reconstruction problem severely ill-posed. Insufficient angular sampling produces streak artifacts and loss of fine structural information. My work in this area investigates **generative and physics-informed reconstruction frameworks** that combine learned image priors with explicit consistency with the measured projection data. Recent work includes dual-stage architectures, attention mechanisms, adaptive feature gating, and differentiable data-consistency steps. **Representative works** - *PIDA-GAN: Physics-Informed Dual-Stage Attention GAN with Data Consistency for Sparse-View CT Reconstruction* — *Physics in Medicine & Biology*, 2026. - *PCDS-GAN: Physics-Constrained Dual-Stage Generative Model with Adaptive Gating for Ultra Sparse-View CT Reconstruction* — IEEE ISBI 2026, Oral. - *Novel Dual-Stage GAN for High-Fidelity CT Image Reconstruction from Sparse-View CT* — JAMIT 2025. --- ### Limited-Angle CT Reconstruction Limited-angle CT reconstruction is challenging because projection measurements are available only over a restricted angular range. The resulting missing information creates direction-dependent artifacts and structural ambiguities that cannot be fully addressed by conventional filtered back-projection alone. I investigate **frequency-aware learning, locality-guided attention, and generative reconstruction** for this problem. These methods are designed to better represent both local image structure and long-range contextual information while learning to suppress artifacts caused by incomplete angular coverage. **Representative works** - *FLoAT-GAN: Frequency- and Locality-Guided Attention for Limited-Angle CT Reconstruction* — AAAI-26 W3PHIAI Workshop, Long Presentation. - *Multi-Frequency and Locality-Guided Attention U-Net GAN for Limited-Angle CT Reconstruction* — IEEE ISBI 2026, Poster; **Best Poster Award: Runner Up**. - *Attention-Guided Frequency and Locality Learning GAN for Limited-Angle CT Reconstruction* — IWAIT 2026 / SPIE Proceedings. - *An Attention-Based Generative Adversarial Network for Limited-Angle CT Reconstruction* — ICCAE 2025; **Best Presentation Award**. --- ### Physics-Guided Deep Learning A recurring methodological theme in my research is the integration of **imaging physics with deep neural networks**. In CT reconstruction, a purely data-driven model may generate visually plausible images without explicitly respecting the measured projection data. I therefore study architectures that incorporate forward and back-projection operators, projection-domain constraints, and data-consistency mechanisms within the learning pipeline. This direction also includes attention-based feature modeling and frequency-domain representations for incomplete-data reconstruction. The aim is to combine the flexibility of deep learning with constraints derived from the acquisition model. **Representative works** - *PIDA-GAN* — *Physics in Medicine & Biology*, 2026. - *PCDS-GAN* — IEEE ISBI 2026. - *FLoAT-GAN* — AAAI-26 W3PHIAI Workshop, 2026. --- ### Electron Tomography Electron tomography presents reconstruction challenges closely related to sparse-view and limited-angle CT, including restricted angular coverage and a limited number of projections. My JSPS fellowship research focused on **ultra-sparse-view electron tomography** and the development of reconstruction strategies that combine model-based methods with learning-based approaches. This work provides a natural bridge between the reconstruction principles developed for X-ray CT and their application to three-dimensional electron-microscopy data. --- ### Model-Based Reconstruction and Level-Set Methods My earlier research focused on **mathematical and optimization-based approaches** to limited-data tomography. In particular, I studied parametric level-set representations for binary and discrete tomography, where object boundaries are modeled implicitly and prior knowledge about image structure is incorporated directly into the reconstruction problem. This work forms an important mathematical foundation for my current interest in hybrid approaches that combine explicit models with learned representations. **Representative works** - *Compressed Sensing-Based Image Reconstruction for Discrete Tomography with Sparse View and Limited Angle Geometries* — *PLOS ONE*, 2025. - *Binary Tomography Reconstruction with Limited-Data by a Convex Level-Set Method* — *Computers, Materials & Continua*, 2022. - *Level-Set Method for Limited-Data Reconstruction in CT using Dictionary-Based Compressed Sensing* — ICCAE 2023. - *New Level-Set-Based Shape Recovery Method and its Application to Sparse-View Shape Tomography* — DMIP 2021. --- ## Methods & Computational Tools | Area | Methods / Tools | |---|---| | Deep Learning | PyTorch, generative adversarial networks, U-Net architectures, attention mechanisms | | CT Reconstruction | Filtered back-projection, iterative reconstruction, sparse-view and limited-angle reconstruction | | Physics-Informed Learning | Forward/back-projection operators, projection-domain constraints, data consistency | | Imaging Software | ASTRA Toolbox, differentiable Radon-transform implementations | | Scientific Computing | Python, MATLAB, Mathematica | | Optimization | Compressed sensing, sparsity-based reconstruction, level-set methods | --- ## Current Research Interests My current interests include **physics-guided generative reconstruction**, **sinogram-domain learning**, **frequency-aware and attention-based architectures**, and methods for reconstruction from extremely limited measurements. I am also interested in extending these ideas toward multimodal and foundation-model approaches for medical imaging, while maintaining a clear connection to the underlying imaging physics.
